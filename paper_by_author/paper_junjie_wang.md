- [RIDAR: Safeguarding Research Integrity in Autonomous Research Agents via Automated Detection and Runtime Intervention](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31372v1)
    - Jiening Siow, Amy Xin, Junjie Wang, Ziyu Tang, Zijun Yao, Yunjia Qi, et al.
    - 📅 Date: October 22, 2026
    - 📑 Publisher: arXiv
    - 💻 Topic: [Misc]
    - 🔑 Key: [agent], [automation], [framework], [benchmark], [dataset]
    - 📖 TLDR: Autonomous research agents are increasingly used to automate large portions of scientific research, yet they are prone to research integrity violations (RIVs): behaviors that compromise the validity, reproducibility, or faithfulness of the research process. We present RIDAR, a framework for automated RIV detection and runtime intervention. First, we introduce RIVT, a comprehensive taxonomy of 14 RIV types spanning the full research lifecycle. Building on RIVT, we present RIDAR-post-hoc, a framework to detect RIVs within agent trajectories. RIDAR-post-hoc achieves 85.2% F1 performance on RIV-Gold, a benchmark for RIV detection with 60 expert-annotated trajectories. Applying RIDAR-post-hoc to RIV-Wild, a dataset of 300 agent trajectories across 50 distinct research tasks, we detect 3,964 RIV instances in 99.7% of trajectories, revealing the prevalence of RIVs in current agents. Finally, we present RIDAR-runtime, a framework for dynamically detecting and correcting RIVs during agent runtime. RIDAR-runtime effectively reduces agent RIVs remaining at task completion by 36.8%. We will release our code and data to facilitate future research.
- [RxnBench: A Multimodal Benchmark for Evaluating Large Language Models on Chemical Reaction Understanding from Scientific Literature](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23565)
    - Hanzheng Li, Xi Fang, Yixuan Li, Chaozheng Huang, Junjie Wang, Xi Wang, Hongzhe Bai, Bojun Hao, et al.
    - 🏛️ Institutions: TIB Leibniz Information Centre for Science
    - 📅 Date: December 29, 2025
    - 📑 Publisher: arXiv
    - 💻 Topic: [Scientific Discovery]
    - 🔑 Key: [llm], [automation], [benchmark], [reasoning], [discovery]
    - 📖 TLDR: The integration of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) into chemistry promises to revolutionize scientific discovery, yet their ability to comprehend the dense, graphical language of reactions within authentic literature remains underexplored. Here, we introduce RxnBench, a multi-tiered benchmark designed to rigorously evaluate MLLMs on chemical reaction understanding from scientific PDFs. RxnBench comprises two tasks: Single-Figure QA (SF-QA), which tests fine-grained visual perception and mechanistic reasoning using 1,525 questions derived from 305 curated reaction schemes, and Full-Document QA (FD-QA), which challenges models to synthesize information from 108 articles, requiring cross-modal integration of text, schemes, and tables. Our evaluation of MLLMs reveals a critical capability gap: while models excel at extracting explicit text, they struggle with deep chemical logic and precise structural recognition. Notably, models with inference-time reasoning significantly outperform standard architectures, yet none achieve 50\% accuracy on FD-QA. These findings underscore the urgent need for domain-specific visual encoders and stronger reasoning engines to advance autonomous AI chemists.
